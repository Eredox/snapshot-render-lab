> Depending on the feature, content submitted to an authenticated AI feature of the Services may be processed on Eredox-operated infrastructure or sent to an external AI provider engaged by Eredox, currently DeepSeek, using credentials held by Eredox; that provider may process it outside Australia, including in the People's Republic of China. Where Customer configures and explicitly enables an external AI connector using Customer's own credentials, Customer Data submitted to that feature is processed by that third-party provider under the terms Customer has agreed with it. The NOVA AI Information Assistant on Eredox's public website is separate from the Services: it is powered by DeepSeek, cannot access Customer Data or any account, and is described in section 61 of the Privacy Policy.

**Privacy s61 "AI providers" (new opening; the closing sentence about training not being asserted either way is kept):**
> Public website information assistant. The NOVA AI Information Assistant on www.nova.eredox.com answers general questions about NOVA from Eredox's public website content. It is powered by DeepSeek (a third-party AI provider) through an account held by Eredox. When you ask it a question, the text of your question, the website page you are viewing and a random visit identifier are sent from our website server to DeepSeek together with excerpts of our public website content. DeepSeek may process this information outside Australia, including in the People's Republic of China, and handles it under its own terms and privacy practices, which Eredox does not control. Do not enter personal information, confidential information, customer data, credentials or security details into the assistant. It has no access to any NOVA account, tenant or customer data. Eredox does not write the text of your question to its own logs; your questions and the answers shown are kept in your browser's session storage for the visit only, and the website application uses your network address temporarily and in memory only to apply abuse limits and the seven-question allowance (standard web-server access logs record network addresses for every page request). Authenticated NOVA AI features. Depending on the feature, content submitted to an authenticated AI feature of the Services may be processed on Eredox-operated infrastructure or sent to an external AI provider engaged by Eredox, currently DeepSeek, using credentials held by Eredox, and may be processed outside Australia, including in the People's Republic of China. Where a Customer configures and explicitly enables its own connector to an external AI provider (for example, OpenAI, Anthropic, or DeepSeek), content submitted to that feature is processed by that provider under the terms the Customer itself has agreed to when configuring the connector. Full detail is in the NOVA AI Privacy Processing Register. Eredox does not represent that any external provider trains models on submitted content, or that it does not — this has not been verified provider-by-provider and is not asserted either way.

**Cookie Policy:** the public site now states that the assistant uses one sessionStorage key (`nova.assistant.v1`: visit identifier, questions asked, answers shown; cleared when the tab or window closes), sets no cookie, and (new paragraph under Third-party technologies) that the assistant sends the question, page and visit identifier to DeepSeek, which may process them outside Australia including in China, that users must not submit personal or confidential information, and that it cannot see any NOVA account or tenant data.
